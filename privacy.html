Privacy notice

A summary of the privacy posture of this static public information website.

This website provides public information, approved announcements and media materials relating to Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade.

Information you provide

The website currently has no server-connected contact form. If a form or other data-collection feature is introduced, this notice should be updated before activation.

Cookies and third-party services

The site is designed as a static website. Hosting, content-delivery, external links or embedded services may be subject to their providers’ privacy practices. Any analytics, advertising or tracking services added later must be disclosed.

Images and corrections

Photographs should be authorised for publication, accurately captioned and accompanied by available credits. Use contact details confirmed by the authorised media office to request a correction.

Review

Review this notice against the actual production configuration and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements before introducing new personal-data processing.
