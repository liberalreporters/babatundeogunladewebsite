Biography

Meet Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS, is a corporate leader, financial professional and progressive politician. He is the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for Ibarapa East Constituency in the Oyo State House of Assembly election. Born on 20 August 1980 in Eruwa, Oyo State, his public profile reflects an interest in grassroots development, party participation and community advancement.

His professional experience spans manufacturing, civil construction, real estate and financial auditing. Across these fields, he has worked in accounting, internal control, finance management and executive leadership—experience he says he hopes to bring to public service through careful scrutiny of public resources, constructive advocacy and engagement with constituents.

Early life & educational background

Ogunlade's education began at Apode High School in Eruwa. His subsequent studies focused on accounting, progressing from national and higher national diplomas to undergraduate and postgraduate qualifications.

The Ogunlade Blueprint for Ibarapa East

A practical plan for representation, opportunity and lasting progress.

Ibarapa East deserves a voice that is heard, a representative who is present, and a plan that turns promise into progress.

This blueprint sets out the priorities Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, APC candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly, intends to pursue on behalf of Ibarapa East Constituency.

A message to the people

Our future should be built together.

Our land is rich in farmland, heritage, enterprise and talent. Our people are hardworking, proud and ready. What the constituency needs is representation that brings these strengths into the rooms where decisions are made, and keeps them there.

This blueprint is a statement of intent and a public commitment. It describes the priorities the candidate intends to pursue, the way he proposes to serve, and the standards by which constituents can assess his work. It is offered in the spirit of an honest conversation and should be shaped by the voices of the people it seeks to serve.

The case for active representation

A constituency prospers when its representation is active, accessible and accountable. The people of Ibarapa East need a lawmaker who listens first, speaks clearly in the House of Assembly, and follows through at home.

Communities have reasonable expectations: better connections to markets, stronger schools, accessible healthcare, safer surroundings and meaningful opportunities for young people to earn a living. Progress requires steady leadership, constructive engagement and the ability to work with relevant institutions at every level of government.

Working across institutions

The value of constructive alignment

As an APC candidate, Hon. Ogunlade proposes to use appropriate party, government and institutional relationships to identify relevant programmes, partnerships and infrastructure opportunities for Ibarapa East. Alignment can create channels for engagement, but it does not guarantee funding or project delivery. Every opportunity must be pursued on its merits and in the public interest.

The blueprint is guided by a simple belief: the constituency's voice should be heard wherever decisions affecting its future are made. Cooperation should extend beyond partisan lines, with the interests of Ibarapa East placed first and good ideas welcomed from every quarter.

The role of a lawmaker

A member of the Oyo State House of Assembly has distinct responsibilities. This blueprint is organised around four of them:

Representation

Carry the concerns, needs and aspirations of Ibarapa East to the House and bring them to the attention of relevant executive institutions.

Lawmaking

Sponsor and support appropriate legislation that protects citizens, encourages enterprise and strengthens institutions.

Oversight

Examine public programmes and budgets through lawful processes so that resources are tracked and public commitments scrutinised.

Constituency service

Remain connected through accessible offices, regular engagement and clear communication with residents.

Beyond policy, constituents deserve clarity about how their representative intends to conduct public service. These are the standards set out in this blueprint.

A shared task

The future belongs to all of us.

No representative can build a constituency alone. This blueprint invites traditional rulers, community leaders, women's groups, youth associations, farmers, traders, professionals and every concerned citizen to take part. The future of Ibarapa East will be written by all of us, together.

Closing declaration

Listen to the people. Speak for the people. Work for the people.

Ibarapa East stands ready for steady and respectful leadership. Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade offers himself with humility, discipline and a commitment to listen to the people, represent their interests and work for the constituency within the responsibilities and limits of the office he seeks.
